<p>Savr is a lightweight, privacy-focused bookmark manager for Android. Paste or share any URL and it automatically fetches the page title, description, and preview image — no accounts, no cloud, no ads, no trackers. Your bookmarks live on your device, where they belong.</p><p><b>Features</b></p><ul><li><b>Auto Metadata</b> — Paste any URL and Savr grabs the title, description, and preview image automatically.</li><li><b>Clipboard Detection</b> — Copy a link anywhere and Savr offers to save it instantly, with a preview before you add it. Already-saved links are recognized, not duplicated.</li><li><b>Quick Save</b> — Share a URL to Savr from any app to save it fast, without even opening the app.</li><li><b>Edit Bookmarks</b> — Open any bookmark in a full-screen editor with live preview and update its title, description, and metadata on the spot.</li><li><b>Collections</b> — Group bookmarks without nested folders. Name it, throw links in, move on.</li><li><b>Selection Mode</b> — Long-press a bookmark, then batch delete or move multiple at once.</li><li><b>Quick Preview</b> — Tap any bookmark to see what's inside before opening it in your browser.</li><li><b>Search &amp; Sort</b> — Search by title or URL; sort by date added or alphabetically, either direction.</li><li><b>Custom Tap Action</b> — Set a single tap to preview, open in browser, or copy the link.</li><li><b>Grid or List</b> — Cards or a compact list. Flip between them whenever.</li><li><b>Export &amp; Import</b> — JSON or HTML, including bookmark exports from Chrome, Firefox, and other browsers.</li><li><b>Auto Backup</b> — Automatic daily backup to your Downloads folder.</li><li><b>Material You</b> — Matches your wallpaper, with a true-black AMOLED theme that saves battery. Light, dark, or system default.</li></ul><p><b>Privacy</b></p><p>Savr has nothing to track you with. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no cloud — everything stays on your device. Even crash reports never leave your phone: if something goes wrong, the full report is saved locally and you can review or share it from Settings → About → Crash logs.</p><p>Savr is free, open source, and licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0.</p>
